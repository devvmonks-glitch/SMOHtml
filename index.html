Hop Lab

Find all 5 gems. Each one needs a different move.

Jump chain
Jump again right as you land, while running, for a double and triple jump
Long jump
Run, hold Crouch, then Jump
Backflip
Stand still, hold Crouch, then Jump
Side flip
Run, reverse direction, Jump during the skid
Ground pound
Crouch in the air. Jump on landing to bounce high
Dive
Press Cap during a ground pound
Cap jump
Throw your cap, then jump or dive into it
Wall jump
Fall against a wall, then Jump
Roll
Hold Crouch and press Cap. Press Cap again to speed up

Keyboard: WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to crouch, E for cap, R to restart. Drag the screen to turn the camera.
