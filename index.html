Hop Lab

dev room

A movement test room. Every station hides a Power Moon that needs a different move, object or capture.

Jump chain
Jump again right as you land, while running, for a double and triple jump
Long jump
Run, hold Crouch, then Jump
Backflip
Stand still, hold Crouch, then Jump
Side flip
Run, reverse direction, Jump during the skid
Ground pound
Crouch in the air. Jump on landing to bounce high
Dive
Press Cap during a ground pound. Jump on landing to roll out
Cap jump
Throw your cap, then jump or dive into it. Hold Cap to keep it hovering
Wall jump
Fall against a wall while holding toward it, then Jump
Ledge grab
Jump at a ledge and hold toward it. Jump to climb, move to shimmy
Roll
Crouch + Cap (or tap Crouch then Cap, or Cap as a ground pound lands). Cap again to speed up, faster downhill
Spin jump
Spin the stick (or press Q), then Jump: a higher, floaty spinning jump
Twirl
Spin the stick (or press Q) in the air to spin and float down slowly
Spin throw
Spin the stick (or press Q), then Cap: Cappy circles around you
Captures
Hit a frog, Goomba or spark pylon with your cap to take control. Crouch to let go
Frog
Hops around and jumps far higher than you can
Goomba
Land on other Goombas to stack them into a tower. The tower stays stacked when you let go
Spark pylon
Zips you along its wire to the other end
Jump flower
Land on it to launch. Jump or pound as you land for extra height
Blocks
Hit ? blocks from below. Bricks break from below or with a ground pound
Post
Ground pound it into the floor
P switch
Step on it for a short-lived staircase
Pipes
Stand on one and Crouch, or ground pound onto it, to warp
Checkpoints
Touch a flag to respawn there with R
